Convert WhatsApp Voice Notes to MP3

WhatsApp voice notes are usually saved as .opus or .ogg files, which some players and forms will not accept. Convert them to MP3 in a few clicks.

How to do it

  1. Save the voice note from WhatsApp to your phone or computer.
  2. Open the Audio Converter and drop the file (up to 20 MB).
  3. Leave MP3 selected and keep 128 kbps, which suits speech.
  4. Click Convert audio and download your MP3.

Good to know

  • MP3 plays on almost every phone, computer and editing app.
  • Need smaller files? Choose 64 kbps for voice. Need better quality? Choose 192 kbps.
  • You can convert to WAV, M4A, OGG, FLAC or WMA instead if another app needs that format.
  • The file is sent to the server for conversion and returned to you.
