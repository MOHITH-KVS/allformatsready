How to do it

  1. Open the Passport Photo Generator and choose a size: Passport 35×45 mm, Visa 25×35 mm, College 30×40 mm, US Visa 51×51 mm, Aadhaar 40×40 mm or Job Portal 35×35 mm. You can also enter a custom size.
  2. Upload your photo (JPG, PNG, WebP or HEIC).
  3. Adjust the zoom and crop, and choose the background colour.
  4. Click Generate Passport Photo and download as JPG or PNG.

Good to know

  • Sizes shown are common ones. Always follow the instructions of the office or portal you apply to.
  • Use a plain, evenly lit photo with your face centred and looking at the camera.

Frequently asked questions

What is the size of an Indian passport photo?

35×45 mm is the commonly used size, which is the default preset. Check your application instructions to be sure.

Is the quality good enough to print?

Photos are generated at 300 DPI.

Does it upload my photo?

No. The generator runs in your browser, so your photo is not sent to a server.
