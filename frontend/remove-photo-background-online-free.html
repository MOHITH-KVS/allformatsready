Remove the Background from a Photo

Many forms ask for a photo on a plain white or light background. The Background Remover cuts out the background and lets you pick a new one, such as white, blue, grey or transparent. Smart mode uses AI on our server, and Plain mode stays on your device.

Open the Background Remover →

How to do it

  1. Open the Background Remover and drop a JPG, PNG or WebP photo (up to 10 MB).
  2. Choose a method: Smart AI for photos of people, or Plain background if the photo was taken against a wall or sheet.
  3. Pick the new background: transparent, white, blue, grey or any colour.
  4. Click Remove background, check the preview, then download a PNG (keeps transparency) or a JPG.

Good to know

  • Smart mode sends your photo to our server, where an AI model cuts out the person and returns a transparent PNG. Plain background mode never leaves your device. If the AI model is not ready yet (it loads when the server wakes), the server uses a basic method and the tool tells you.
  • Plain background mode works best on an evenly coloured wall or sheet. If too much or too little is removed, move the Sensitivity slider.
  • Check the edges, especially hair, before you submit the photo. If the result is not clean, retake the photo in good light against a plain wall.
  • For a passport photo, choose a white background and download the JPG. Then use the Photo & Signature Resizer if the form asks for a size in KB.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get a white background for a passport photo?

Drop the photo, choose a method, pick the white swatch and click Remove background. Download the JPG.

Is my photo uploaded?

In Smart mode, yes: the photo is sent to our server to be processed and returned to you. In Plain background mode it stays on your device. Avoid Smart mode on a shared computer or for documents you do not want to upload.

Does it work on every photo?

It works best on clear photos with one person or a plain wall behind the subject. Busy backgrounds or hair against similar colours can leave rough edges.

Is it free?

Yes. No sign-up and no watermark.
