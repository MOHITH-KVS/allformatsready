Compress an Aadhaar PDF Under 200 KB

Banks and government portals often accept Aadhaar only under a small size, such as 200 KB. Upload the file once to AllFormatsReady and you get a JPG under 200 KB plus other sizes and formats, so you can download only the one the portal needs.

How to do it

  1. Open the converter on the home page and drop your Aadhaar PDF or image (up to 10 MB).
  2. Click Generate All Formats. It takes roughly 5 to 15 seconds.
  3. Download the JPG under 200KB card, or use the PDF Organizer if the portal needs a PDF under a size you choose.
  4. Is your e-Aadhaar PDF locked? Use the Unlock PDF tool first, then convert it.

Good to know

  • If your e-Aadhaar PDF is password-protected, open it with the password and save a copy without one first. The converter cannot read locked PDFs.
  • A masked Aadhaar hides the first 8 digits and shows only the last 4. For text-based PDFs the converter can report when it finds an Aadhaar number and offer a masked version. Scanned images are not redacted automatically.
  • Check what the portal asks for: some want JPG, some PDF, and the size limit varies.
