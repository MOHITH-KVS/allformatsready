Convert a Word (DOCX) File to PDF

Upload a .docx file to the AllFormatsReady converter. It is rendered to a PDF first, then you can download it as PDF, JPG, PNG or WebP.

Open the converter →

How to do it

  1. Drop your .docx file (up to 10 MB) into the converter.
  2. Click Generate All Formats.
  3. Download the PDF or any other format you need.

Good to know

  • This is a best-effort rendering of text, headings, lists, tables and pictures. Complex layouts can differ slightly from Word.
  • Text in complex scripts such as Hindi or Telugu may not render correctly. For those, use Word's File, Save as PDF and upload the PDF instead.
  • Old .doc files are not supported. Open them in Word and save as .docx or PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Does the layout match Word exactly?

Not always. It is a best-effort rendering, so check the result.
