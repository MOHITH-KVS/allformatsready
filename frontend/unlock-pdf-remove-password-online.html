Remove the Password from a PDF (Unlock PDF)

Many portals cannot read a PDF that is password-protected, and e-Aadhaar, e-PAN, bank statements and payslips often arrive locked. If you know the password, this tool gives you an unlocked copy you can upload anywhere.

Open Unlock PDF →

How to do it

  1. Open Unlock PDF and drop your locked PDF (up to 10 MB).
  2. Type the PDF password.
  3. Click Unlock PDF.
  4. Download the unlocked copy. It opens without a password.

Good to know

  • This tool needs the correct password. It cannot guess or recover a forgotten one, and you should only unlock files you own or have the right to open.
  • e-Aadhaar PDFs commonly use the first 4 letters of your name in capitals plus your year of birth, for example RAJE1990. e-PAN PDFs commonly use your date of birth as DDMMYYYY. Check the instructions that came with your file, because formats can differ.
  • Your file and password are sent to the server over HTTPS to remove the lock. Avoid using this on a shared computer. If you cannot accept that, open the PDF in your own PDF viewer and print it to a new PDF instead.
  • After unlocking, you can compress the PDF to a size or convert it to JPG.

Frequently asked questions

How do I remove the password from a PDF?

Open Unlock PDF, drop the file, type its password and click Unlock PDF. You then download a copy that opens without a password.

What is the password of an e-Aadhaar PDF?

It commonly is the first 4 letters of your name in capitals followed by your year of birth, such as RAJE1990. Check the instructions that came with your file.

Can it unlock a PDF if I forgot the password?

No. It only removes a password you already know.
