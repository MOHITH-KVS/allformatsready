Resize a Photo or Signature to Exact KB and Pixels

Exam, bank and job forms often demand a photo or signature between a minimum and maximum size in KB and at a fixed width and height. This tool does the resizing on your own device, so your picture is never uploaded.

Open the Photo & Signature Resizer →

How to do it

  1. Open the Photo & Signature Resizer and drop a JPG, PNG or WebP (up to 10 MB).
  2. Pick a preset such as SSC, IBPS / SBI or NEET / JEE, or type your own width, height, minimum KB and maximum KB.
  3. Choose Crop to fill or Fit on white if your picture has a different shape.
  4. Click Resize & compress, check the preview and download the JPG.

Good to know

  • Presets are typical values, not official rules. Always check your form's latest notification and edit the fields if it differs.
  • Only need a smaller file? Leave width and height empty and use a chip such as Under 50 KB. The tool keeps the shape and lowers the size.
  • If a form needs a minimum size and your small picture comes out lighter than that, the tool adds harmless metadata to the JPEG to reach it, and tells you it did so.

Frequently asked questions

How do I reduce a photo to 50 KB?

Drop the photo, click the Under 50 KB chip and press Resize & compress. The tool finds the best quality that stays under 50 KB.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. The resizing runs in your browser, so the picture stays on your device.

Which formats can I use?

JPG, PNG and WebP as input. The result is a JPG.

What if it cannot reach the size?

The tool says so and shows what it achieved. A simpler photo, or larger pixel dimensions if the form allows, usually fixes it.
