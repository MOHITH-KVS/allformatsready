Hide Aadhaar and PAN Numbers on an Image

Sometimes you must share a copy of a document but not the full number. The Mask Sensitive Info tool covers the digits with a black box or pixelation, entirely in your browser, so the image never leaves your device.

Open the Mask tool →

How to do it

  1. Open the Mask tool and drop a JPG, PNG or WebP picture of your document.
  2. Drag a box over each number you want to hide, or press Auto-detect PAN / Aadhaar (beta).
  3. Check the preview. The download unlocks once at least one area is covered.
  4. Click Download masked image.

Good to know

  • Choose Black box for anything sensitive. Pixelation can sometimes be partly reversed on small text.
  • Auto-detect reads the text on your device and can miss blurry, tilted or low-contrast numbers. Always check the preview and add boxes by hand where needed.
  • A masked Aadhaar normally hides the first 8 digits and keeps the last 4. Mask only what the recipient does not need.
  • The first auto-detect run downloads about 10 MB of text-reading data, then it works on your device.

Frequently asked questions

Is my image uploaded?

No. Drawing boxes and downloading happen in your browser. Auto-detect also runs on your device after loading its text reader.

Why is the download button locked?

It unlocks after you cover at least one area, so you never download an unchanged copy by mistake.

Does it work on PDFs?

The Mask tool works on images. For text-based PDFs, the main converter reports Aadhaar numbers it finds and can provide a masked version.
